# Proposal: GDPR Compliance — Health Inc

**Date:** 2024-07-22
**Prepared by:** Priya Sharma
**Client:** Health Inc (Healthcare)
**Project type:** compliance
**Duration:** 9 weeks
**Total price:** $95,000

## 1. Background
Health Inc serves patients in the EU and must comply with GDPR. Consent records and data subject requests are handled manually across three systems.

## 2. Scope
- Consent management: record and honor patient consent changes
- Data subject requests: access and erasure workflows
- Audit logging for all consent and erasure events
- Data retention policy and automated cleanup

## 3. Approach
1. Discovery and data mapping (3 weeks)
2. Real-time consent tracking module (3 weeks)
3. Data subject request workflows (2 weeks)
4. Audit logging and handover (1 week)

## 4. Timeline
Start: 2024-08-26. End: 2024-10-25. Duration: 9 weeks.

## 7. Risks
- **Legacy system performance (medium):** Consent changes will be checked in real time against the existing consent database.
- **DPO sign-off timing (medium):** The client's Data Protection Officer must approve the design.

## 8. Assumptions
- The legacy consent database can support real-time lookups at expected peak load
- DPO available for two review sessions
